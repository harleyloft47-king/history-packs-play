# History Packs

A free pack-opening card game: collect 1,408 cards of real museum art and objects, every one public domain and made before 1900.

**Play:** https://harleyloft47-king.github.io/history-packs-play/

Works on phones and computers. Your collection is saved in your own browser. Coins are free in-game coins only and have no real-world value.
