# History Packs — Art credits

Every artwork in History Packs is a public-domain work made before 1900. Each image file comes from a
source that releases that file as CC0 or public domain: The Met Open Access, National Gallery of Art
Open Access and Smithsonian Open Access (CC0), and Wikimedia Commons for 2D paintings marked
public domain (PD-Art) with a named source. Card lines in the game are our own writing.

Images are downloaded once and bundled locally in `public/art/` (WebP, longest side 512 px); the game never
hotlinks. The curated list is `public/art/credits.json` (a copy of `refs/real-art/credits.json`).
